# Petrel Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Türkçe](CODE_OF_CONDUCT_TR.md)

## Community standards

Treat others with respect. Give constructive feedback, focus criticism on the work, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable.

These rules apply to participation in Petrel's GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report violations privately to a project maintainer using the contact details available on their GitHub profile. Include a link to the relevant conversation if you can. For content that violates GitHub's rules, you can also use GitHub's reporting tools.

Maintainers will limit sharing of reports to what is needed to review and address them. Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation.
