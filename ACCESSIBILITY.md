# Petrel Accessibility

[English](ACCESSIBILITY.md) · [Türkçe](ACCESSIBILITY_TR.md)

## Using the printer display

Petrel's LCD interface uses the printer's rotary encoder. Rotate it to move through menus or adjust a value, and press it to select. Some actions require holding the encoder. See the [Petrel documentation](docs/README.md) for screen and control instructions.

## Current limitations

The LCD interface presents information visually and relies on the physical encoder for navigation. Petrel does not document screen reader support for the LCD or claim conformance to an accessibility standard.

## Reporting an accessibility barrier

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/sezgynus/Petrel/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) in English or Turkish. Tell us which screen, control, or documentation page you were using, what you were trying to do, and what got in the way. If relevant, include the Petrel version, display model, and any settings or assistive technology involved. Screenshots and photos are optional.
